## Section 1 | Introduction

### 1.1 | Information

This Handbook is available to Head Course Instructors and above. It includes the information they need while doing their daily duties. Leaking this document will result in punishments.

### 1.2 | Roles

Head Course Instructors (HCIs) operate the Officer Training Corps. Their duties include:

- Marking Admission Forms, Security Phase quizzes and Final Exams.
- Handling the Database and event logs.
- Handling recommendations.
- Managing their timezone staff members.
- Hosting Graduations.

**Quota:** 3 points weekly, earned from marking forms and hosting events. Marking 3 Admission Forms or Final Exams grants 1 point.

**Reporting:** Submit HCI Logs daily, unless you have not done anything that day. At the end of the week, HCIs must submit weekly reports.

## Section 2 | Grading

### 2.1 | How?

Follow the Universal Grading Keys provided below. Failure to do so will result in consequences. Each type of submission has its own grading policy, explained in the subsections that follow.

**Plagiarism:** Plagiarized responses receive a score of 0 and are failed immediately. Also issue the individual a 30-day blacklist, except for Security Phase quizzes, where you do not blacklist them.

#### 2.1.1 | Admission Forms

Admission Forms are the entry to the Officer Training Corps and should not be treated like the Final Exam. Their questions are very personalized, so there are no exact wrong answers, and the grading key is not as diverse as the Final Exam's.

- 10 questions, 5 points each.
- Deduct the required points for each mistake.
- A perfect form scores 50 points.

#### 2.1.2 | Final Exams

The Final Exam is the final test in the Officer Training Corps. Grade it more harshly than the Admission Form. It uses the same criteria as Admission Forms, but mistakes cost more.

- 13 questions, 5 points each.
- Deduct the required points for each mistake.
- A perfect exam scores 65 points.

**Percentage score calculation**

1. Add up all the points the Cadet has achieved.
2. Divide the points by 65.
3. The decimal you are given is their percentage score.

Cadets must score **75% or higher** to pass.

| Using abbreviations | -0.5 (per mistake) |
| Missing parts | -2 |
| Going off-topic | -2 |
| Repeating yourself with no purpose | -1 |

| Not handling a scenario properly | -3 |
| Bias | -2 |

The source also lists the line "USES' BRITISH" above "Not restating question" with no deduction attached.

#### 2.2.3 | Security Phase Grading Keys

Cadets must score **24/30** to pass.

| Score | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 24-30 | Passed |
| 20-23 | Retake exam |
| 19 or below | Retake phase |

Each question is worth 3 points:

- 1 point for grammar
- 1 point for detail
- 1 point for properly answering the question

If the Cadet does not answer correctly, they do not get the point for detail either.
